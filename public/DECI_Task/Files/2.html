Topic 11.2: Task 1 — Set Up Your Notebook and Explore the Data

Preparing your Colab notebook correctly and taking a first look at the digitamart_orders.csv dataset before any cleaning begins.

📓 Five Steps, In Order
▼

Task 1 is the first task of the project: preparing your notebook and taking a first look at the data. It has five steps, and they should be done in order.

Task 1 Roadmap
Steps 1–2
  • Set up the notebook
  • Load the data
Steps 3–4
  • Check the data's size
  • Describe what you see
Step 5
  • Share the notebook
🏷️ Naming and Loading
▼
1 Set Up the Notebook

Open Google Colab and create a new notebook. Name it exactly as required: StudentID-EgyptData — your student ID number, followed by that word.

⚠️
The Name Matters

The exact name keeps your work distinct from every other student's and easy to identify when it is reviewed.

At the top of the notebook, create a text cell and write your name, your ID number, and the name of the project. Think of this cell as the cover of your work — the first thing anyone sees.

2 Load the Data

Load the file digitamart_orders.csv into a table called a DataFrame. A DataFrame is the structure that pandas uses to work with data — rows and columns, just like any table.

After loading it, display a preview of the first few rows. Looking at this preview with your own eyes confirms the data loaded correctly and that no error appeared.

📐 Shape and Columns
▼
3 Check the Data's Size

Find out how many rows and how many columns the dataset has. The rows represent the number of orders, and the columns represent the information recorded about each order.

Also print out the names of every column, so that you know exactly what you are working with before you start.

💡
Why This Comes Before Anything Else

Knowing the size and the column names orients the rest of your work. You cannot clean or analyse a column you have not confirmed exists.

📝 Describing What You See
▼
4 Describe the Data

Write a text cell containing a short description, three to five sentences, in your own words. Say what the data is about, what each row represents, and identify at least one numeric column and one text column.

✅
The Goal Is Understanding, Not Just Loading

The point of this step is to prove that you understand the data in front of you — not just that you managed to load it.

🔗 Sharing the Notebook
▼
5 Share the Notebook

Share your notebook and set the permission to Anyone with the link — can view, so that anyone with the link can open and see your work.

⚠️
Keep the Link

You will submit this link at the end of the project, so save it somewhere you will not lose it.
