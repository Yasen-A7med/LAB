Topic 11.1: Project Overview — Meet DigitaMart

An introduction to the term project: the scenario, the dataset, and the three rules every notebook must follow.

🛍️ Meet DigitaMart
▼

The term project replaces the written exam. Instead of sitting a traditional test, every student builds one complete project, start to finish. Everyone works on the same project — there are no alternative topics or choices. The brief is identical for the whole class.

ℹ️
The Scenario

Imagine you are working as a data analyst at an online store in Egypt called DigitaMart. The store sells many products to customers across different cities, and new orders are recorded every day.

DigitaMart's management wants to understand the numbers behind the business so they can make better decisions: which products bring in more money, which cities buy more, and whether customers are satisfied. Your job in this project is to take the store's order data, clean it, and analyze it to produce useful information for the business.

🗂️ The Dataset
▼

The dataset is a file called digitamart_orders.csv. Each row represents one order that a customer purchased from the store. The file has ten columns.

order_id
A unique number for each order.
order_date
The date the order was placed.
city
The customer's city. Five cities appear: Cairo, Alexandria, Giza, Mansoura, and Aswan.
product_category
The type of product, such as Electronics, Clothing, Books, and Food & Groceries.
product_name
The name of the product itself.
order_total
The total amount the customer paid for the order, in Egyptian pounds.
delivery_minutes
How long delivery took, in minutes.
customer_rating
The customer's rating of the order, from one to five.
payment_method
How the customer paid, such as Cash, Credit Card, Wallet, and Bank Transfer.
customer_age
The customer's age.
⚠️
This Is Real-World Data

Like any real dataset, this data has problems that need fixing before analysis can begin. Discovering and resolving those problems, step by step, is exactly what the project teaches.

📐 The Three Rules
▼

All of the work happens in a single place: Google Colab. That means one notebook, with everything inside it — no other tool is used. There are three important rules to follow throughout the whole project.

Rule 1
Name the Notebook Correctly
The notebook name must be exactly StudentID-EgyptData — your student ID number followed by that word.
Rule 2
Add an Intro Text Cell
At the top of the notebook, write a text cell containing your name, your ID number, and the name of the project.
Rule 3
Every Result Must Come From a Cell
Any result you show must come from a cell that actually ran — never typed in by hand. After every step, add a short Markdown note explaining what you did and what the result means.
